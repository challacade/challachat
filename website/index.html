2

Start Capturing Chat

Run the ChallaChat executable and follow the simple setup:

1. Launch challachat.exe from the extracted folder

2. Enter the URL of any YouTube livestream when prompted

3. ChallaChat will automatically start capturing chat messages and display the overlay URL

That's it! No browser extensions or complex setup required.

3

Add to Streaming Software

Add the chat overlay to your streaming software:

In OBS Studio, Streamlabs, or your preferred streaming software, add a new Browser Source.

Use the overlay URL provided by ChallaChat (typically something like: http://localhost:7072)

Adjust the width, height, and placement of the source for your stream. Chat messages will appear in real-time!

4

Customize Your Overlay

Personalize your chat overlay to match your stream:

In your streaming software, right-click the overlay source and select Interact to access the settings menu.

Choose from several appearance presets, customize colors, fonts, and animations to match your stream's branding perfectly.
